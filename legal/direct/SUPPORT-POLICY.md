# Support Policy

**On the Rise Digital** — Alison Risner
Last updated [DATE]

> Not legally required, but it earns its place. Saying what support is
> prevents the two expensive outcomes: a buyer expecting a help desk, and a
> buyer who assumes there is no support and refunds instead of emailing.
>
> Set expectations you can actually meet. One person answering within two
> business days, honestly stated, beats "24/7 support" that is not true.

---

## How to get help

Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. That is the only channel — there is no phone line
and no live chat, because there is one of us.

## What to include

Send these and the problem is usually solved in one reply:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. What happened instead, exactly — the error text, or a screenshot.
3. Your Windows version, and the product version from **About**.
4. The relevant part of the activity log, if the product has one.

## Response times

We aim to reply within **[2 business days]**, usually sooner. Hours are
[MON–FRI, 9–5 STATE TIME]. We are not available on US public holidays.

If something is badly broken for many people, we will say so publicly at
[WEBSITE] rather than answering the same email a hundred times.

## What support covers

- Installing, activating and running the software.
- Diagnosing errors and crashes.
- Working out whether something is a bug, and fixing it if it is.
- Replacing a lost licence key.
- Advice on using features the documentation covers.

## What support does not cover

- **General computer help** — Windows problems, drivers, antivirus
  configuration, network setup.
- **Third-party services.** If a site changes how it works, we may be able to
  adapt the software, but we cannot support their service.
- **Advice on whether you may download something.** We cannot give legal
  advice. See the Terms of Use.
- **Custom development** or features built for one customer.
- **Unsupported systems** — anything older than the stated requirements.
- **Modified installations**, or copies obtained anywhere but from us.

## Bugs

Report them. A clear bug report with a log is the most useful thing a
customer can send, and we would rather know. Fixes go into the next release;
anything serious gets one sooner.

We cannot promise a fix for every bug, particularly where the cause is in a
third-party component or a site we do not control.

## Feature requests

Welcome, and genuinely read. We cannot promise to build any of them, and a
missing feature is not grounds for a refund — see the
[Refund Policy](REFUND-POLICY.md).

## Updates

Fixes and minor updates for the version you bought are free. See section 10
of the [Terms of Sale](TERMS-OF-SALE.md) for how major versions work.

Direct-download builds do not update themselves — check [WEBSITE] for new
releases, or [subscribe to release notifications].

## End of life

If we stop supporting a product we will say so at least **[90 days]** ahead,
and the final version will stay downloadable for existing customers for at
least **12 months** after that. Software you have bought keeps working
regardless; there is no kill switch and nothing to phone home to.

## Languages

Support is in English only.
