## The short version

**Digital products are delivered immediately and all sales are final.** We
make exceptions where the law requires one, and where the software genuinely
does not work and we cannot fix it. Talk to us before asking for your money
back — almost every problem is solvable, usually the same day.

## 1. Why sales are final

You receive the complete, working product the moment you pay. There is
nothing to return, and nothing we can take back. A refund on digital software
means you keep the product and we return the payment, which is why the
default across the industry is that sales are final.

## 2. When we will refund you anyway

**2.1 It does not work and we cannot fix it.** If the software will not run
on a system that meets the stated requirements, and we cannot resolve it with
you within a reasonable time, we will refund you in full. **Give us the
chance first** — tell us what happened and send the log, and we will usually
have an answer within a day.

**2.2 You were charged twice**, or charged the wrong amount. Refunded in full
without question.

**2.3 You never received it.** If delivery failed and we cannot get the
product to you, you get your money back.

**2.4 Within 14 days in the EU or UK**, where you have not waived the right —
see section 4.

**2.5 Where consumer law otherwise requires it.** Your statutory rights are
not affected by anything in this policy.

## 3. When we will not

**3.1 You changed your mind**, or bought it by accident, after downloading.

**3.2 You did not read what it does.** The product page, the documentation
and the free material describe the software accurately and in detail. Check
before buying, and ask us anything beforehand — we would rather answer ten
questions than process one refund.

**3.3 A third-party website or service stopped working with it.** The
software depends on services we do not control. Those services change, and
sometimes break things. That is a limitation we state plainly before you buy;
it is not a defect.

**3.4 You want it to do something it does not do.** Feature requests are
welcome. They are not grounds for a refund.

**3.5 Your computer does not meet the requirements**, which are listed on the
product page before purchase.

**3.6 You breached the licence** — shared your key, redistributed the
software, or used it in a way the EULA forbids.

## 4. EU and UK buyers

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK you normally have **14 days** to cancel
a distance purchase.

That right does not apply to digital content you have consented to receive
immediately. **At checkout you will be asked to tick a box** confirming that
you want immediate access and understand you are giving up the right to
cancel. If you tick it and download, the 14-day right no longer applies.

Prefer to keep it? Do not tick the box. Email ontherisedigital@gmail.com and we will
hold delivery for 14 days instead.

## 5. How to ask

Email **ontherisedigital@gmail.com** with:

- the email address you bought with
- the order number or approximate purchase date
- what went wrong

We reply within **2 business days** and process approved refunds within
**5 business days**. The money returns by the method you paid with; your
bank may take several days longer to show it.

## 6. Chargebacks

Please come to us first. A chargeback costs us the sale and a fee on top, and
takes weeks to resolve, where an email usually takes hours.

We contest chargebacks where the product was delivered as described and we
were given no chance to help. We may decline future sales to accounts that
charge back without contacting us.
