## Choosing between them

**Version A is better.** A site with no analytics needs no consent banner, no
cookie management, and no ongoing compliance work, and the notice fits in a
paragraph.

If you do want visitor numbers, use a **cookieless, privacy-first** analytics
service — Plausible and Fathom are the usual choices, roughly $9–14/month,
EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant by design. They set no cookies at all, which means
you can keep Version A and still get your numbers. That is strictly better
than Google Analytics plus a consent banner, and it is one of the few places
where the cheap option is also the correct one.
