## 1. What Easy-dlp is

Easy-dlp is a desktop front end for the open-source downloader `yt-dlp`. You
give it an address, and it saves what is at that address to a folder on your
computer. That is the whole of it.

**It is a tool, not a service.** It does not host, index, supply, recommend or
provide access to any content. It has no catalogue, no search, and no
connection to us. It cannot reach anything you could not already reach
yourself in a browser, signed in as yourself.

## 2. What it is for

Easy-dlp exists so that you can keep a local copy of material you are
entitled to keep a local copy of. Typical and intended uses include:

- Course videos and materials you have **bought or subscribed to**, saved for
  offline viewing.
- **Accessibility.** A local file can be slowed down, captioned, magnified,
  colour-adjusted, or played in a media player that suits a vision or hearing
  impairment, where a browser player cannot.
- Material you created yourself, or that is in the public domain, or that is
  offered under a licence permitting download.
- Keeping access to something you paid for after a subscription lapses, where
  the terms of that service allow it.
